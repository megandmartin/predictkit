The live event is Crypto World's Fair. Its official rules set the deadline at 12 October 2026, 23:59 Pacific. In Astana this corresponds to 13 October at 11:59 (UTC+5); aim to submit earlier.

Each entrant may belong to one team, and a team may have one project submission at a time. All members register; the team leader submits. Third-party code and ownership must be disclosed. Functionality, impact, novelty, UX, open-source composability and business viability are judging criteria.

## Consequence for this project
Toolkit maintainers may plan a team; workshop participants remain separate teams. Do not add the hosts to each participant submission or claim their differentiated code. Verify how shared starter code, mentor contributions, prior work and organizer/sponsor relationships affect eligibility. The rules exclude some sponsor/administrator-affiliated individuals; co-hosting locally is not itself a verified eligibility decision. Ask Colosseum for a written clarification where relevant. Independent local judges should avoid scoring their own toolkit against participants.

# Pilot measurement, with consent
Record a team identifier rather than personal contact details in the public dataset. Ask permission for any recording, quote or publication.

Fields: toolkit version; runtime/model; start time; initial coding/chain experience; selected template; approved brief time; first useful action time; first chain proof time when present; completion criterion; pass/fail; mentor interventions; model requests/tokens and known cost; errors; next-day continuation.

Primary metric: teams completing the predeclared end-to-end path divided by teams that attempted it. Report both numerator and denominator. Secondary metrics: median time, spread, intervention rate, real chain verification rate and continued use. Track dropped or blocked teams; do not remove them to improve results.

Do not count agent calls, example clicks or scripted rehearsals as startups, customers or on-chain apps. Do not claim a speed improvement without a reasonable comparison. A small workshop pilot is initial usability evidence, not proof of a large market.

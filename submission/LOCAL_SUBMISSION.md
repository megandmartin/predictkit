# Local workshop submission form
Team name and members; project name; user/problem; one differentiated feature; selected template and toolkit version; repository/demo link; observed primary flow; actual network/provider; what is mocked; test/evidence link; pitch summary; source attribution; next milestone; permission to show/record the demo.

A local submission is for this workshop only. Colosseum registration and the official form are separate and remain the team's responsibility. The hosts should not claim ownership or automatically submit participant projects on their behalf.

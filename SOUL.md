# Product principles

We help builders get to something useful and provable. We do not manufacture the appearance of progress.

The founder chooses the problem. The team helps make it concrete. A narrow product that works is better than a beautifully described company that does not.

Be candid about uncertainty, failed tests and external dependencies. Show the next decision, blocker, owner and evidence before long explanations. Surface the most important problem first.

Respect participants as independent founders. They own their differentiated applications; using a starter does not make them employees, cofounders or traction we may claim without consent. Do not publish their briefs, code, recordings or contact details without permission.

Make safe things easy: local rehearsal, clear reset, visible network state, portable files, controlled approval and a stop button. Real-money operation requires its own authority, legal assessment and security work.

This file describes behavior. It is not a credential, a policy engine, an executable runtime or a claim of agent consciousness.
